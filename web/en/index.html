Frequently asked questions

Does NEXO POS handle SST?

Yes. Set one or more taxes (for example SST at 6% and 8%) and choose which each item carries; items with no tax, such as rice or sugar, can be set tax-free. Prices can include tax or have it added, and receipts and the tax report show each rate separately.

Can I take DuitNow QR and card payments?

You can record DuitNow QR, card, e-wallet, cash and bank transfer. The system records the method and amount; the money goes through your usual QR or card terminal, not through the system.

Is cash rounded?

Yes, to the nearest 5 sen as in Malaysia: .01 and .02 round down, .03 and .04 to .05, .06 and .07 to .05, .08 and .09 up. Card and QR are charged to the sen.

Can I sell without internet?

Yes. Sales are kept on the computer while offline and sent automatically when the connection returns.

What hardware do I need?

A computer or tablet with Chrome. A USB barcode scanner and a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printer are recommended. Counting uses an ordinary Android phone.

Does it support NFC and RFID?

Yes. Android phones read NFC tags and member cards; USB NFC readers and UHF RFID handhelds work in keyboard mode with no setup.

I already use Loyverse. Can I use both?

Yes. Paste a Loyverse access token to sync items, stock levels and sales; after a count, correct figures can be written back to Loyverse.

What about StoreHub, Qashier and other tills?

Export a sales report (Excel or CSV) from your till and import it in the app; the columns are recognised automatically.

Is my data safe?

Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase). Each employee sees only what their permissions and branch allow, and every change is logged.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new company gets 14 days free for up to 5 users, with no credit card.

Can it make LHDN e-invoices?

Yes. Each month, Reports → e-Invoice downloads the consolidated e-invoice (MyInvois JSON and CSV) to submit in the MyInvois portal.

A customer asks to delete their data?

Press Remove (PDPA): hidden at once, erased for good after 90 days; old receipts keep the amounts but lose the name and phone, as PDPA 2010 requires.

Are there video guides?

Yes. Every part has step-by-step guides with videos on the website's Guide page, and in the app under Profile → How to use.
