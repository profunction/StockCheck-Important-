Feature breakdown

What's in the box

Three checkout modes

  • F&B: tables, open tickets, add-ons (sweetness, extra egg)
  • Kitchen slips, dine-in / takeaway / delivery, service charge
  • Supermarket: big scan box, type 3* then scan for 3 pieces
  • Retail: tap item photos, categories, barcodes and NFC

At the counter

  • Discounts, members and points
  • Hold and recall bills
  • Weighed items and quick keys for bread or vegetables
  • Keeps selling offline; uploads when the internet is back

Back office

  • Sales by item, category, staff, payment method, add-on
  • Shift records with cash-drawer count
  • Tax and service charge, receipts, branches
  • QR table ordering, LHDN e-invoice, API access tokens
Who uses it

Four people, one system

Cashiers

A fast till that guides them with a built-in ▶ tutorial on every screen.

Shop managers

Shifts, cash counts, discounts and staff, branch by branch.

Owners

Every sale lands in JSOX Every Data and JSOX Books without re-typing.

Kitchen

Orders print straight to the kitchen with their add-ons.
