Feature breakdown

What's in the box

Stock in and out

  • Receiving against purchase orders
  • Transfers between locations, with stock in transit
  • Adjustments: damaged, expired, found
  • Reservations and returns to supplier

Buying

  • Purchase orders with approval
  • Reorder suggestions turned into a PO
  • Pinduoduo buying
  • Landed-cost calculator and supplier list

Control

  • Head office, warehouse and shops in one list
  • Count differences from SuXin Barcode Scan
  • Month-end stock
  • Activity log of every movement
