Feature breakdown

What's in the box

Sales and e-invoice

  • Invoices, credit notes and debit notes
  • MyInvois e-invoice JSON, then record the LHDN UUID
  • Company TIN, registration, SST and MSIC on every invoice

Money in and out

  • Receive money against invoices
  • Pay supplier bills
  • Expenses: rent, utilities, fuel
  • Customers and suppliers with balances

Reports and control

  • P&L, balance sheet, trial balance, ledger
  • Who owes us (aging) and who we owe
  • SST report, PDF or CSV
  • Journal, chart of accounts, period lock
Who uses it

Four people, one system

Owners

Invoice, get paid and see profit without learning accounting.

Accountants

Double-entry journal, chart of accounts, period lock.

Admin staff

Record bills, expenses and payments as they come in.

Customers

Proper invoices and e-invoices, every time.
